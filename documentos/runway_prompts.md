# Runway — Biblioteca operativa condensada

No es canon. Si contradice `00_...`–`05_...` o `skill_*.md`, prevalecen estos últimos.

## Background continuity
Use the attached empty-background reference as the authoritative source for every object,
surface and piece of equipment. Use the anchor-with-characters image only to determine
initial character placement and pose. Do not invent, restyle, relocate or add background objects.

## Camera
The camera remains stable and controlled. Internal cuts and changes of framing are allowed when
they support the beat. Use medium shots, reaction close-ups, simple inserts, gentle push-ins and
soft pull-backs. Avoid handheld motion, aggressive zooms and unnecessary lateral moves.

## Character clip template
**REFERENCES:** background, character sheets, anchor, screen assets.  
**STYLE:** approved SimuLENS 2D editorial style; preserve palette/texture; no extra grain/noise.  
**FORMAT:** vertical 9:16, 720p.  
**SCENES:** describe 2–3 internal scenes when useful, each with framing, action, emotion and dialogue.  
**AUDIO:** dialogue + music + foley/SFX.  
**END:** specify final pose/frame for continuity.

## Explainer template
Cream background, navy typography, restrained coral/teal accents, generous empty space,
minimal motion and very subtle paper texture.

Use 2–3 clear beats:
1. current problem;
2. SimuLENS mechanism;
3. professional control / benefit.

Generate VO, music and SFX in the same 15–20 s clip.
