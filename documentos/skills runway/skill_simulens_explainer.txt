# SimuLENS Explainer

## Purpose
Use for Phase 2: the calm explanatory motion-graphic block between the problem and the happy path.

## Default format
**15–20 seconds, one single generated clip** containing:
- animation;
- voice-over;
- music;
- sound effects.

Vertical 9:16, 720p.

## Narrative job
The explainer must answer three things clearly:
1. Why does the current method/tool fall short?
2. What specific mechanism does SimuLENS add?
3. What does the professional still review, adjust, validate or decide?

Do not invent the problem, product capability or claim. Use the approved episode concept.

## Visual language
Warm SimuLENS editorial motion graphics:
- cream background;
- large navy typography;
- very few simple icons/shapes;
- restrained coral and teal accents;
- generous empty space;
- smooth minimal transitions;
- very subtle paper texture;
- no extra grain/noise.

Keep one idea per card/beat.
Avoid dense diagrams, formulas, photorealism, glossy 3D, complex medical animation, floating parameters or full clinical interfaces.

## On-screen text
Short, large and readable.
Voice-over should complement the text, not repeat it word-for-word.

For critical wording, product names, numbers, tables, QR codes or complex graphics, use a prepared reference asset rather than asking the video model to improvise them.
Simple generic icons do not require a separate asset unless visual continuity demands it.

Brand spelling must always be **SimuLENS**.

## Voice-over
Warm, calm, professional and concise. No urgency.
Spoken pronunciation:
- topographer → **tuh-POG-ruh-fer**
- SimuLENS → **SIM-yoo-lenz**
Visible text keeps standard spelling.

## Music and SFX
Music should be soft, calm and reassuring, clearly less comic than Phase 1.
Use subtle transitions, soft wipes/chimes and restrained SFX to support comprehension.

## Prompt structure
1. approved problem + solution mechanism;
2. visual style;
3. duration / 9:16 / 720p;
4. Beat 1: current limitation;
5. Beat 2: SimuLENS mechanism;
6. Beat 3: professional control / concrete benefit;
7. exact on-screen text;
8. full voice-over;
9. music + SFX;
10. final transition toward Phase 3.

Prefer a simple, communicative explanation over technical completeness.
