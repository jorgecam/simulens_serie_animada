# SimuLENS Character Scene

## Purpose
Use for narrative scenes with recurring characters, mainly Phase 1 and Phase 3.

## Visual rule
Use approved visual references as authoritative.
Style: **warm cinematic 2D editorial animation**, organic dark outlines, soft painted/cel shading, very subtle paper texture, muted teal/coral/cream/navy/blue-grey/warm wood.

**Preserve the exact texture and palette of the supplied reference images. Do not add extra grain, noise or texture that is not present in the reference.**

## Reference hierarchy
Before writing the generation prompt, identify the references actually needed:
1. **Empty background** = authoritative environment, objects, machinery, palette and layout.
2. **Character sheet** = identity, face, hair, clothing and proportions.
3. **Anchor / first frame** = starting position, pose and framing.
4. **Screen asset** = exact monitor/tablet/phone/TV content when precision matters.
5. **Specific prop asset** = any important object not already defined.
6. **Previous final frame** = continuity when a direct visual handoff is needed.

Never reconstruct hidden background objects from an anchor containing characters.

## Phase defaults
### Phase 1
18–22 s total, normally **2 generated clips**.
Each clip may contain **2–3 scenes / camera setups**. Seedance 2.5 can handle internal cuts and reframing; do not fragment into microclips without a reason.

### Phase 3
10–15 s total, normally **1 generated clip**.
Use 2 only for a clear location change, narrative jump or technical continuity need.

## Camera
Stable and controlled. Medium shots, reaction close-ups, simple inserts, clean internal cuts, gentle push-ins/pull-backs.
Avoid shaky camera, orbiting, aggressive zooms and unnecessary lateral movement.

The unit of clarity is the **beat**, not the file. Each beat should have one clear action and emotion.

## Dialogue
Keep dialogue verbatim unless a phonetic spelling is required for speech.
Do not overlap character voices unless explicitly requested.
If the line cannot fit naturally, shorten the script or increase duration instead of accelerating speech.

Speech pronunciation:
- topographer → **tuh-POG-ruh-fer**
- SimuLENS → **SIM-yoo-lenz**
Visible text keeps normal spelling.

## Music and SFX
Default character-scene music: light, comic and instrumental, with **pizzicato / marimba / ukulele** as preferred colors because they match the established series.
Keep it subtle and professional, never childish or slapstick-heavy.
Use restrained punctual SFX/foley; do not stack comic effects.

## Prompt structure
1. authoritative references;
2. visual style;
3. duration + vertical 9:16 + 720p;
4. short environment note;
5. internal scenes / beats in order;
6. character actions, expressions and verbatim dialogue;
7. camera changes/cuts;
8. music + foley/SFX;
9. critical continuity restrictions;
10. end pose/frame if needed for the next clip.

## Screens
Do not rely on the video model for dense or exact clinical UI, numbers, PDFs, spreadsheets, QR codes or critical text. Use prepared screen assets when accuracy matters.
