# SimuLENS Characters & Continuity

## Purpose
Canonical casting and identity reference for Runway Agent. Use it to choose who belongs in a scene and to prevent character drift.

**Approved character images are authoritative.** If text and the latest approved asset differ, follow the asset.

## Casting
- **Emma** — clinic, fitting, professional decision.
- **Sam** — laboratory technical coordination / support.
- **Martin** — specialized lens design and design judgement.
- **Nora** — manufacturing, machines, physical inspection.
- **Carl** — adult patient, father of Mia.
- **Mia** — pediatric patient / family dynamic.
- **Helen** — regulation, traceability, GDPR, CE/FDA.

When several laboratory characters appear:
**Sam coordinates/interprets; Martin designs; Nora manufactures/inspects.**

## Emma
Warm, bright, capable optometrist. Concern reflects workload/time/risk, never incompetence.
Warm brown skin, short curly black hair, dark rectangular glasses, small earrings, white clinical coat over coral blouse, dark trousers.

## Sam
Young laboratory specialist; precise, calm, competent, friendly, slightly deadpan.
Short black hair, **single-ear support headset with microphone**, navy polo with **Meridian Lab** logo, dark trousers.
Never negligent, hostile or old-fashioned.

## Martin
Older specialist lens designer; creative, meticulous, relaxed, slightly unconventional.
Almost bald with grey side hair; reading glasses **resting on top of his head, low toward the forehead**; light patterned/paisley shirt with rolled sleeves, dark trousers.
Never a relic of the past.

## Nora
Efficient, meticulous laboratory operator.
Middle-aged Asian woman, dark hair tied back, transparent safety glasses, very pale light-blue/blue-grey laboratory work jacket with **Meridian Lab** logo, light grey inner layer, dark trousers. **No tablet.**
Never clumsy or disorganized.

## Carl
Adult **African-American patient**, father of Mia. Reasonable, mildly skeptical when the process is unclear, relieved when resolved.
Warm brown skin, short tight black curls, short stubble, olive-green long-sleeve shirt, beige trousers, brown shoes.
Never laboratory staff or the target of the joke.

## Mia
Carl’s **African-American daughter, about 10 years old**. Must read clearly as a child, not a teenager.
Warm brown skin, curly black hair in a high ponytail behind the head, muted coral/pink top, olive/khaki trousers, light shoes with coral accents.
Never the target of the joke.

## Helen
55–65, serious, professional, restrained and slightly imposing; never a villain.
Short dark-grey hair, rectangular glasses, very upright posture.
Variants:
- laboratory inspection: **CE/FDA** accreditation;
- clinic inspection: **GDPR** accreditation.
Identity stays the same; clothing/accreditation may change with context.

## Continuity rules
1. Character sheet = **who the character is**.
2. Anchor = **where/how the character starts the shot**.
3. Never invent changes of age, relationship, profession, clothing or identity.
4. Preserve face, hair, proportions and approved wardrobe across clips.
5. SimuLENS supports; the professional decides.
